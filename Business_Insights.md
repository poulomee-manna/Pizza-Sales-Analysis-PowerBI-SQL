# 📊 Business Insights

## Executive Summary

This analysis provides valuable insights into pizza sales performance by examining customer ordering behavior, product performance, and sales trends. The dashboard helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions to improve business performance.

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Peak Sales Period

- Weekend evenings recorded the highest number of customer orders, indicating increased demand during this period.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend

- March recorded the highest order volume, while February had comparatively fewer orders.

### 3. Pizza Category Performance

- The Classic pizza category contributed the largest share of total sales and total orders.

### 4. Pizza Size Performance

- Large-sized pizzas generated the highest revenue, indicating a strong customer preference.

### 5. Best Performing Product

- Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest revenue among all pizzas.

### 6. Lowest Performing Product

- Brie Carre Pizza generated the lowest revenue and recorded the fewest orders.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory during weekends to meet higher customer demand.
- Promote high-performing pizzas through combo offers and seasonal campaigns.
- Review pricing and marketing strategies for low-performing products.
- Focus promotional efforts during lower-sales months to improve overall revenue.

---

## Business Value

This dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor key business KPIs.
- Track sales performance over time.
- Identify top and bottom-performing products.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.
