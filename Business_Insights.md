# 📊 Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product-level insights. By combining SQL for data analysis with Power BI for interactive visualization, the dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and make informed business decisions.

The analysis focuses on revenue generation, customer ordering patterns, pizza category performance, size preferences, and best- and worst-selling products.

---

# 1. Revenue Performance

The business generated **$817.86K** in total revenue from **49,574 pizzas sold** across **21,350 customer orders**, resulting in an average order value of **$38.31** and an average of **2.32 pizzas per order**.

These KPIs indicate consistent customer purchasing behavior and provide a baseline for measuring future sales growth.

---

# 2. Customer Ordering Trends

Daily order analysis shows that customer demand is strongest during **weekends**, particularly on **Friday and Saturday**, suggesting increased purchasing activity before and during the weekend.

Monthly trend analysis indicates that **July** recorded the highest sales volume, while lower sales were observed during **October**, highlighting seasonal variations in customer demand.

Understanding these trends helps management optimize staffing, inventory planning, and promotional campaigns.

---

# 3. Pizza Category Performance

Among all categories, the **Classic** category contributed the highest percentage of total sales and customer orders, making it the most popular product category.

The **Chicken** category generated strong revenue despite a comparatively lower order volume, indicating a higher average selling price.

Category-level analysis helps identify customer preferences and supports product portfolio optimization.

---

# 4. Pizza Size Analysis

Large-sized pizzas generated the highest revenue contribution, followed by medium-sized pizzas.

This indicates that customers generally prefer purchasing larger pizzas, providing opportunities for combo offers and upselling strategies.

---

# 5. Best Performing Products

The dashboard identifies the top-performing pizzas based on:

- Revenue
- Quantity Sold
- Total Orders

Products such as **Thai Chicken Pizza** consistently ranked among the highest revenue-generating items, making them suitable candidates for promotional campaigns and featured menu items.

---

# 6. Lowest Performing Products

The analysis also identifies pizzas with the lowest revenue, order count, and quantity sold.

Products such as **Brie Carre Pizza** generated relatively low sales, suggesting the need for pricing reviews, promotional offers, or possible menu optimization.

---

# 7. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can improve business performance:

- Increase inventory availability during weekends to meet higher customer demand.
- Promote best-selling pizzas through seasonal campaigns and combo offers.
- Reassess pricing and marketing strategies for low-performing products.
- Introduce targeted promotions during lower-demand months to maintain consistent sales.
- Use category and size preferences to optimize inventory planning and menu offerings.

---

# 8. Business Value

This dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor business KPIs in real time.
- Identify sales trends and seasonal demand.
- Track product performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
- Improve operational efficiency through interactive reporting.

---

# Conclusion

The Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be combined to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. By leveraging interactive visualizations, KPI reporting, and trend analysis, the solution provides decision-makers with actionable information to improve sales performance, optimize inventory, and enhance overall business strategy.
